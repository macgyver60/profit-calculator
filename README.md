# ProCalc - iPhone-style Profit Calculator

💰 A beautiful, real-time profit calculator with an iPhone-style interface.

## Features

- 📱 Authentic iPhone app design with notch and home indicator
- ✨ Smooth gradient UI with purple theme
- 🎚️ Interactive sliders for percentage inputs
- 📊 Real-time calculation as you adjust values
- 💵 Formatted currency display (M for millions, K for thousands)
- 📈 Breakdown showing overhead vs profit contribution
- ✅ Pre-filled with example values

## How It Works

Calculate your profit share with 4 inputs:
1. **Overhead** - Total overhead costs
2. **Revenue** - Total revenue
3. **Your Overhead Share %** - Your percentage of overhead
4. **Your Profit Share %** - Your percentage of profit

### Formula
```
Your Profit = (Overhead × Overhead Share %) + ((Revenue - Overhead) × Profit Share %)
```

## Example
- Overhead: $3,000,000
- Revenue: $4,000,000
- Your Overhead Share: 14%
- Your Profit Share: 8%

**Result:**
- From Overhead: $420,000 (3M × 14%)
- From Profit: $8,000 (1M × 8%)
- **Total: $428,000**
